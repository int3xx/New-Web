Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how INT3X handles information when you use the application and its services.

1. Information We Collect

INT3X may process information required to provide its Discord features, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, configuration settings and moderation-related information.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To provide and operate INT3X features.
  • To save server configuration settings.
  • To provide moderation and management functionality.
  • To maintain and improve the service.

3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in the application's database in order to provide persistent server settings and features.

4. Data Sharing

INT3X does not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be processed when necessary to operate the application or comply with applicable requirements.

5. Discord

INT3X operates through Discord. Your use of Discord and its services is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6. Data Removal

If you want information associated with your server or use of INT3X to be removed, please contact the INT3X administration.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the INT3X administration.
