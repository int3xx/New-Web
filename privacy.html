This policy explains how INT3X handles information when you visit this website.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only when needed to operate the site or comply with the law.

5. Removal

To have a message you sent removed, contact us and we will delete it where possible.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always published on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact form on the home page.
